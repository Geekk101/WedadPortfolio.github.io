Master’s thesis: a curious AI worm

Emergence of curiosity-driven behavior in a biologically inspired C. elegans agent under the Maximum Occupancy Principle (UPF Barcelona, 2026). With no task reward, the agent explores, finds food and avoids running out of energy. Below is a simplified illustration, not the thesis simulator.

Energy
Food captured0
Area explored0

Click the arena to drop food. The curious worm wanders into unvisited space and heads for food when its energy runs low. The reward-driven worm goes straight to the food.

  • After eating, the agent kept exploring: about 19 new cells visited before the next food source.
  • With five food items, 3 of 10 episodes collected all five and half collected at least four.
  • It reached food around an obstacle with no retraining. A reward-trained swimmer captured far more food, a trade-off I discuss in the thesis.
